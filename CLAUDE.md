# CLAUDE.md

## Design Context

This project has a strategic design brief at [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md) and a visual system spec at [DESIGN.md](./DESIGN.md). **Read both before changing any UI.**

Quick summary:
- **Register:** brand (marketing-first). The public site converts first-time visitors into bookings; admin/POS surfaces serve users after that.
- **Personality:** cinematic, mysterious, dramatic — film-noir poster, not Halloween horror.
- **Primary user:** Thai players (with EN/中 fallbacks) on mobile, discovering scripts to book.
- **Anti-references:** generic SaaS landing, cheap horror motifs, boardgame retailer look, gaming/esports neon.

Brand colors and typography are locked in DESIGN.md frontmatter — do not introduce new accent colors or font families without updating it.
